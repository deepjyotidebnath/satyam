A local favourite on N.N. Road

Hotel Satyam Sundaram is a family restaurant in the heart of Cooch Behar, serving hearty North Indian, Bengali and Chinese food at honest prices — famous for its unlimited rice specials served with 3 pieces of chicken.

  • Unlimited rice thalis from ₹100
  • Fresh naan & kulcha from the kitchen tandoor
  • Paneer butter masala, chowmein, egg roll & more
  • Dine-in · Takeaway · Online ordering
